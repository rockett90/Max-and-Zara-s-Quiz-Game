Instructions and Controls

    Help our cheerful crab scuttle along the sandy seabed on a brilliant underwater adventure!

    Use the Right Arrow key to move forward – but be careful, rocks and seaweed are scattered about, and if you bump into them, they’ll slow you down.

    To avoid these obstacles, use the Space Bar to make the crab jump over them.

    When you see a mysterious question box floating high above, press the Up Arrow to fire a bubble from the crab’s bubble-blasting claw.

    Pop the box open and answer the question correctly to earn points – the higher the number on the box, the more points you’ll receive!
